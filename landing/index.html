Recording, production, and a bench that keeps it running.

Make Believe Studio is a working studio. We record and produce, we look after a room full of instruments and outboard gear, and we repair it ourselves when it stops behaving.

The studio

Gear that is used, not displayed

Amplifiers, microphones and outboard that earn their place by being tracked, maintained and fixed rather than admired. Every piece is catalogued, its location known, and its repair history kept — so when something fails mid-session we know what it is, where it has been, and what was last done to it.

We take in third-party gear too. A customer's amp is logged, photographed on arrival and given its own tag, so its condition when it reached us is a matter of record rather than of memory.
